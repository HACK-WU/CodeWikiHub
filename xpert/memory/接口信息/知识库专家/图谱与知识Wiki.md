【接口信息｜GraphRAG 图谱与知识 Wiki】\n- 子功能：图谱进度/重建/查询与手工修正、Wiki 状态/配置/页面、Wiki 目录与分类\n- 所属模块：知识库专家\n- 接口列表：\n  - `/api/knowledgebase/:id/graph`（`GraphragController`，**无声明式守卫**，写操作内调 `assertKnowledgebaseWriteAccess`）：`GET documentStatus`/`documentStatuses`・`POST documents/:documentId/retry`・`POST rebuild`・`GET status`（`error` 为空即正常）・`GET catalog`・`GET visualization`・实体族 `GET entities`・`POST entities`・`PATCH entities/:id`・`DELETE entities/:id`（**软隐藏**）・`GET entities/:entityId/neighborhood`・`GET entities/:entityId/chunks`・关系族 `GET relations`・`POST relations`・`PATCH relations/:id`・`DELETE relations/:id`・`GET mentions`；批量状态入参有数量上限校验\n  - `/api/knowledgebase/:knowledgebaseId/wiki`（`KnowledgeWikiController` + `KnowledgeWikiOrganizationController`，无声明式守卫）：`GET status`（含不确定调用计数与 `recoveryActions`、`canManage`）・`PUT config`・`GET documents/status`・`POST rebuild`（**非空 Wiki 需确认计费**）・`POST jobs/:jobId/retry`（仅失败任务；不确定调用需额外确认）・`GET pages`・`GET pages/:pageId`（带 placement）・目录族 `GET|POST|PATCH|DELETE folders*`・`PATCH taxonomy`（启停分类）・`GET classificationStatus`・`GET|POST classification-runs*`（含 `apply`）・`GET graph`（分类图）\n- 主要错误：`Error.KnowledgebaseWiki{PageNotFound,StateReadOnly,Disabled,RebuildChargeConfirmationRequired,RetryChargeConfirmationRequired,JobRetryInvalid,OrganizationInvalid,OrganizationConflict,FolderNotEmpty}`\n- 关键约束：图谱/Library 的写操作用 service 断言；Wiki 重建与重试在「不确定调用」存在时要求计费确认
